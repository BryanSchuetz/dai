Against a backdrop of escalating demand for land, a dual tenure system, underdevelopment in the land sector, and low tenure security, Zambia’s Ministry of Land and Natural Resources (MLNR) faces a pressing need to establish baseline land values and conduct a comprehensive National Land Audit that will deliver accurate, timely, and complete land data, enabling better decision making and more effective land management practices.

The Support to the National Land Audit project supported the design and implementation of the National Land Audit, working with the MLNR and the Ministry of Local Government and Rural Development to develop and test audit methodologies for implementation in select districts.

Part of the EU’s Global Gateway initiative, the project helped Zambia build the reliable land data needed for more transparent and efficient land management. At the closing event in November 2026, EU Head of Partnerships Paolo Sergio formally handed over the audit’s findings, data, and systems to the Government of Zambia.

The project succeeded in delivering better land records and access to information for citizens, stronger planning and decision making tools for government, and greater transparency for investors. As William Nyundu, Director of Planning and Information and Acting Permanent Secretary, said at the handover event: “A land audit matters only if a farmer can get a document for her plot, if a council can collect its revenue, and if an investor knows which land is available and on what terms. That is the work ahead, and the Ministry is ready for it.”

## Sample Activities

* Develop a cadastral map of large-scale land-based investments.
* Improve the functioning of the current National Spatial Data Infrastructure (NSDI) and update it with harmonized information (cadastral data) and new information layers (current land use, including recent high-resolution satellite imagery).
* Build the capacity of district authorities through on-the-job training to implement district-managed audit tasks.
* Test and validate methodologies for various audit tasks and implement land audits in up to 20 districts.
* Produce a national action plan for further unpacking and rolling out the National Land Audit in all 116 districts, including a national training and capacity-building initiative.

## Select Results

* National Land Audit dashboard developed for the MLNR
* NSDI policy approved by Zambian Cabinet
* 68.2 million hectares (90.6 percent of the nation) captured in a new land-use map
* 25.5 million hectares of state land mapped and audited
* 3,966 large-scale land investments audited
* 22 districts audited across all 10 provinces
* 750,000 agricultural and settlement parcels digitized
* 116 district land-use maps developed and available for download
* 58 data layers published on the NSDI portal
* 55 new datasets added to the NSDI (target 12)
* 289 participants trained, including 190 enumerators in 19 districts and 15 GIS operators
