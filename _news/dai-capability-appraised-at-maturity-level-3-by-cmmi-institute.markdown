DAI is delighted to announce that it has maintained its [CMMI Maturity Level 3](https://cmmiinstitute.com/learning/appraisals/levels) rating, following similar appraisals in [2019](https://www.dai.com/news/dai-appraised-at-cmmi-maturity-level-3) and in [2022](https://www.dai.com/news/dai-re-appraised-at-maturity-level-3-by-cmmi-institute).

The Capability Maturity Model Integration (CMMI) framework, originally created for the U.S. Department of Defense to assess the quality and capability of its contractors, is a capability improvement framework that provides organizations with the essential elements of effective processes that ultimately improve their performance.

The CMMI Institute appraised DAI’s business development unit at CMMI Level 3 for Services. An organization appraised at Maturity Level 3 is performing at a level where processes are well characterized, clearly understood, and described in defined standards, procedures, tools, and methods.

![cmmi-news.jpg](/uploads/cmmi-news.jpg)

“We’re extremely pleased to secure the Level 3 rating for another three years,” said DAI’s Jeremy Kanthor, who served as the internal champion for the appraisal. “This accomplishment speaks to our rigorous processes and company-wide commitment to continuous improvement in the services we can bring to our customers.”

The [CMMI Institute](https://cmmiinstitute.com/company) “enables organizations to elevate and benchmark performance across a range of critical business capabilities, including product development, service excellence, workforce management, data management, supplier management, and cybersecurity.”
