DAI is delighted to submit its 2021 Communication on Progress to the United Nations Global Compact.

The [Compact](https://www.unglobalcompact.org/) is a “voluntary initiative based on CEO commitments to implement universal sustainability principles,” with a mission to “mobilize a global movement of sustainable companies and stakeholders to create the world we want.” 

![Screen Shot 2022-01-17 at 4.59.12 PM-831d0a.png](/uploads/Screen%20Shot%202022-01-17%20at%204.59.12%20PM-831d0a.png)

<!--more-->

Member firms align strategies and operations with 10 principles covering human rights, labor rights, environmental sustainability, and ethical business practices, and seek to advance social progress as articulated in the [17 UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)](https://sdgs.un.org/goals). DAI joined the Compact in 2018.
