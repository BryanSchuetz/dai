DAI is pleased to announce that it has been awarded a place on the One Acquisition Solution for Integrated Services Plus ([OASIS+](https://www.gsa.gov/oasis-plus/about/)) Multi-Agency Contract. Holding this contract qualifies DAI to compete for prospective work in a range of service areas across any U.S. Government department or agency.

<!-- more -->

![oasis-plus.png](/uploads/oasis-plus.png)
 
Administered by the General Services Administration (GSA), OASIS+ is designated by the Office of Management and Budget as a best-in-class contracting vehicle, making it a preferred means of procuring services across the federal government. OASIS+ holders are considered thoroughly vetted, highly qualified contractors. The GSA’s evaluation criteria for this award include demonstrated use and achievement of performance management strategies, for example, and consistent third-party validation.
 
The contract scope is organized by “domains” or groupings of related services. DAI was awarded four service domains: Management & Advisory, Technical & Engineering, Research & Development, and Environmental.
 
“We are delighted to secure a place on OASIS Plus,” said Jeremy Kanthor, who will serve as DAI’s OASIS+ Program Manager. “We look forward to competing in a range of areas where DAI’s long record of technical excellence and assured program management position us to provide exceptional value for the U.S. Government and the U.S. taxpayer,”
 
OASIS+ is the successor to the OASIS contracting mechanism, also held by DAI, whose ordering period ended in March 2025. Inquiries to DAI related to OASIS+ may be sent to [oasisplus@dai.com](mailto:oasisplus@dai.com)
