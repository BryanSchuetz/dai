In collaboration with FT Longitude, DAI has published a report on how governments are building the market integrity and quality needed to scale nature-based carbon trading.

*Unlocking the Potential of Carbon Markets* explores how carbon market can unlock climate finance in developing countries. The report explores requirements to establish robust, transparent and credible carbon market mechanisms that can secure international climate finance and draws on case studies from Colombia, Ghana, Bhutan, and Thailand.
