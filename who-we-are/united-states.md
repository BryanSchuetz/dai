# United States

From its earliest days as a start-up in 1970, DAI has maintained a presence in or near Washington, D.C. The founders’ office was a tiny establishment in Georgetown. Today, our office in Bethesda, Maryland, just outside Washington, is home to scores of employees, including our CEO. This location offers proximity to many of our U.S. and international clients, as well as partner organizations, think tanks, educational institutions, and other focal points for the international development and finance community. With its exceptional global transportation infrastructure, Washington is also the operating base for many of the leading technical experts and support staff critical to the success of our projects worldwide—from compliance and contracts specialists to finance, information technology, human resources, and project management professionals.
