Our culture is built around engagement, collaboration, and excellence. An environment where all staff are connected to our mission and values is an environment where everyone can contribute fully to our goal of shaping a more livable world.

## Our Commitment

As it says in our values, we are committed to treating “everyone, everywhere with professionalism and dignity.” We work hard to ensure that all employees are welcomed, valued, and rewarded on their merits, and that all employees—regardless of background or any aspect of their identity—enjoy equal opportunity for advancement. 

Organizationally, our Workforce Engagement team is part of the Ethics & Integrity office, which aims to ensure high standards of ethics and excellence are integrated into every aspect of our business, wherever we operate.

## Professional Resource Groups

Professional Resource Groups are an integral part of DAI, providing opportunities for employees to connect, collaborate, and grow within a supportive community tailored to their needs. These groups afford networking opportunities, support professional growth, and ensure all employees have the resources they need to succeed.
