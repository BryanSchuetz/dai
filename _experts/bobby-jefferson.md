Bobby Jefferson is a member of the senior management team at DAI. He joined the firm in 2016 and has served in various roles, including as Vice President and Chief Technology Officer for the company’s global health division, where he was instrumental in establishing DAI’s successful digital and global health teams.
 
In 2020, he joined the Board of Directors of the Society for International Development-Washington, D.C. Chapter, a position to which he was re-elected in 2021 and 2024. As a member of SID-Washington’s Executive Committee, Bobby has helped to shape the organization’s strategic thinking on various issues, including innovation and inclusive approaches to development. He has held similar advisory roles for the Council of International Development Companies and British Expertise International.
 
Before joining DAI in 2016, Bobby served as Vice President and Director of the Center for Development Informatics at Palladium (formerly Futures Group), where he built a practice focused on ICT for global health and adjacent sectors, leading a staff of 120 in 10 countries.
 
He serves as a board member and advisor for enterprises including THINKMD, ClickMedix, DataElevates, Covelocity.Health, SimPrints, the Mentor Capital Network, and the gBeta MedTech Black Founders Accelerator program.

* B.S., computer science, American University
* At-Large Member, Board of Directors, SID, United States
