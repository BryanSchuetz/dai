Jose Larios is a fiscal and public finance expert with more than 30 years of experience advising ministries of finance and sub-national governments around the world on budget planning and expenditure, tax policy, and tax administration.  

At the Inter-American Development Bank, Jose led public policy dialogue on fiscal and financial issues and influenced policy design and reforms throughout Latin America and the Caribbean. He successfully promoted modernizations in tax systems, fiscal decentralization, public investment systems, designed innovative solutions such as the Smart Fiscal Ecosystem to increase efficiency and equity in government spending, developed a series of micro-simulation models to design tax policy reforms, spearheaded municipal finance reforms, and developed a fully integrated financial management system. 

Prior to the IDB, Jose was a senior consultant and project manager responsible for designing and implementing donor-funded fiscal reform projects in Africa, Eastern Europe, the former Soviet Union, and Latin America. In these roles, he gained extensive experience with U.S. Agency for International Development projects. 


* Master's degree, public policy, Harvard University
* M.A., applied economics, San Francisco State University
* B.A., economics, University of California, Berkeley
