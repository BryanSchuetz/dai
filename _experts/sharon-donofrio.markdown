Sharon D'Onofrio is the Senior Director of Monitoring, Evaluation, and Learning (MEL) for the USAID Africa Trade and Investment Activity (ATI), a program that supports businesses across 50 African countries in accessing export markets and investment capital. She joined DAI in 2020 as the MEL Director for USAID INVEST, a global initiative focused on driving private investment in sectors such as sustainable agriculture, renewable energy, women-led businesses, and financial services. Sharon's diverse experience includes leading a global learning network, serving as a learning and training consultant, and managing a microfinance institution in Latin America.

B.A., political science, University of Arizona
M.A., international and public affairs, University of Pittsburgh
