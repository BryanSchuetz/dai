Kayte Meola is a sociologist specializing in women’s and youth’s engagement within the agriculture and environment sectors in international development. She is currently a Lead Specialist with DAI’s Resilience and Stability Practice and brings more than 20 years of professional and academic experience in research in agricultural value chains and natural resource management, education, violence reduction programming, strategic planning, program design, capacity building, resource development, new business development, and communications. 
 
She has conducted studies on the differential impacts of natural resource management and agriculture interventions in Brazil, Colombia, and Honduras and has worked on projects in Afghanistan, Dominican Republic, Democratic Republic of Congo, Ecuador, El Salvador, Guatemala, Honduras, Jamaica, Jordan, Puerto Rico, Suriname, Uganda, Uruguay, and Vietnam. Her passion is integrating violence prevention programming into development initiatives to strengthen girls’ and women’s abilities to realize their full human potential.
