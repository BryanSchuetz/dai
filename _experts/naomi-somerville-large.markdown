Naomi Somerville-Large leads the Green and Inclusive Growth pillar in DAI U.K.'s Economic Growth and Climate Resilience team, where she brings expertise in rural agriculture, smallholder livelihoods, market systems development, financial inclusion, and women's economic empowerment. With extensive field experience across South Asia, the Middle East, and East, West, and Southern Africa, Naomi has over 13 years of expertise in designing and delivering impactful programs. Her work spans government donors—including the UK Foreign, Commonwealth & Development Office, the German International Cooperation Society (GIZ), and the Finnish Ministry of Foreign Affairs—and major global corporations such as Mars Wrigley, Mondelez International, Unilever, The Coop, and Aldi, during her tenure with Fairtrade. Before rejoining DAI, Naomi served as Senior Technical Lead for Mars Wrigley and the Finnish Ministry of Foreign Affairs £10 million program in Côte d'Ivoire, aimed at enhancing cocoa farmers' incomes through improved financial access, income diversification, and cooperative professionalization.

* M.A. history and politics, University of Edinburgh
* M.A. globalization and multinational enterprises, School of Oriental and African Studies (SOAS)
