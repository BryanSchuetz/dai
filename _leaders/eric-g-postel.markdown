Eric Postel is a senior advisor to DAI’s Chief Executive Officer and leads the Office of the CEO, overseeing functions such as communications, corporate strategy, and impact and sustainability analysis. 

Prior to joining DAI, Eric was a Senior Advisor to the Administrator of the U.S. Agency for International Development (USAID), working on USAID’s collaboration with the private sector in Latin America and the Caribbean as well as on the COVID-19 response. He previously served at USAID as Associate Administrator, Assistant to the Administrator for Africa, and Assistant Administrator for the Bureau of Economic Growth, Education, and Environment. Eric also served as USAID’s "Plus 1" (alternate director) to the Millennium Challenge Corporation. He is the creator of the Global Alliance for Trade Facilitation, a public-private partnership that facilitates trade by streamlining processes to move goods across borders. And he is one of the six creators of Power Africa, which has mobilized $54 billion in private sector commitments by 210 private sector partners, with more than 160 transactions closed in 42 countries to provide more than 15,000 megawatts of power.

Eric is the founder of Pangaea Partners, an investment banking and consulting firm focused on emerging markets. Its accomplishments include helping six African countries create or improve their stock markets, modernizing more than 20 former Soviet banks, and advising on creating the Slovak Export-Import Bank. He was also one of the founders of Zambia’s leading stockbroker which was sold 10 years later to its local employees. 

Before Pangaea, Eric worked as a Vice President at Citibank Tokyo. He also has five years of experience working in biotechnology and providing capital to clean energy start-ups and energy-efficient projects in Wisconsin. Between his undergraduate and graduate studies, he served in the United States Navy.

* M.B.A., Stanford University
