Natawnee Fritz oversees functions including corporate strategy and policy, security, information technology, and AI enablement.

![Natawnee Fritz](/uploads/natawnee-leader-photo.jpg)

Natawnee previously served as Vice President, Business Transformation, where she managed corporate strategic initiatives—such as the digital transformation of DAI’s finance and accounting systems and development of our monitoring, evaluation, and learning capabilities—and led our U.S. Government business unit’s strategy function.

For the two years prior, she was a Vice President at GeoPoll, a U.S. small business focused on mobile-based research in emerging and frontier markets, where she led the international development and humanitarian assistance unit.

Natawnee originally joined DAI in 2009. She has held various roles across the company, including serving on the corporate strategy team, building a market intelligence unit, and contributing to economic growth, governance, and health programming. She began her career in international development as a community development volunteer with the Peace Corps in Cabo Verde. She is proficient in Portuguese.

* M.B.A., University of Maryland
* B.A., international relations and comparative religions, Tufts University
